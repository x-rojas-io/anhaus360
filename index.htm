Who We Are

Real Estate has been my passion for the last five years of my life, along the way I’ve been fortunate to meet people and connect with families of different backgrounds at a different stages of their homeownership journey, being able to help them find a place to call home is truly rewarding and achieving. Coming from contribution has been the driving force of my career and encompasses my day to day approach towards my clients and business. A dedicated father and husband is the role I enjoy the most when off the work, I spend with love my free time to my family and friends and some hobbies like biking.

Hernando Trujillo
